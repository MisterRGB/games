How Hang Something? works

Hosting. The host starts a room and shares the 5-digit code (✂ copy). Everyone joins from their own computer.

Random word-setter. Each round a random player becomes the word-setter & artist — not just the host. They pick a secret word and draw the hanging figure. The word box hides what you type — hover to peek and check your spelling, but keep your screen away from other players!

Guess one-by-one. The guessing team takes turns — the game taps each player in turn for one letter. Used letters are crossed off the board.

Drawing. Stamp the gallows (it fills the page; the noose loops the dashed ring — that's the swing pivot) then ink the figure with brush, eraser and colours. With each wrong guess the next body-part is checked off — get creative with every piece.

Win! All letters found and you take the round — the host starts the next one.

Lose. Wrong 8 times and the drawing drops & swings from the noose — the setter wins the round.
