HOW TO PLAY

Sixty seconds to learn, a lifetime to blame the bots.

🎯 THE GOAL

Snatch prize cards to score their points. First to 35 takes the crown. If the deck runs out first, the richest player wins.

🃏 EACH ROUND

A prize card flips over — its number is the points on offer. Everyone secretly picks one card from their hand and locks in before the timer dies. All cards reveal at once and the highest card claims the prize. Everyone locks early? The reveal fires instantly — speed is free pressure.

💥 CLASH & JACKPOT

If the top cards tie (or two ⚡ Bolts meet), they explode: those cards burn, nobody scores, and the prize value rolls into the jackpot — stacking onto next round's prize. Big pots make people greedy. Greed makes stories.

😵 FUMBLES

Fail to lock in before the timer dies and you fumble: your weakest card plays itself, face-up, for everyone to laugh at.

🔄 REFILL

Hands refill to 7 cards every round (8 if you ghosted). Played cards burn — so spending your King on a 2-point prize hurts later.

🕹️ CONTROLS

Tap a card to raise it, tap again (or hit LOCK IN) to play it. Desktop: keys 1–9 select, Enter / Space plays.

🤖 SOLO & MULTIPLAYER

Play 2–5 personality-driven bots offline, or host a table and share the 4-letter code — friends join from any phone or laptop. Cards travel peer-to-peer.
