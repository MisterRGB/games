A CARD GAME OF ELEMENTAL FIRE
PYRE

Match the pile's element or its number. Stack a pyre to its limit and it IGNITES. Chain plays to feed your flame multiplier — but drawing from the deck snuffs it. Each round the Rite deepens: thinner decks, new elements, choking ash, hungrier pyres.

THE RITES

  • ◈A card may be played on a pyre that shares its element or its rank. Empty pyres accept anything.
  • 🜂A full pyre IGNITES: it burns away for bonus points and reseeds. Five of one element is a PURE BURN.
  • ✕ASH matches nothing. It can only be fed to a pyre one card from ignition — and it burns with it.
  • ×8Every consecutive play raises your flame multiplier (up to ×8). Drawing resets it to ash.
  • ★Wild plays anywhere and leaves the pyre open. Echo plays anywhere and draws you a free card. Both grow scarce.
  • ♥Three embers of life. Run dry with no plays and one dies. After every round, THE CRITIC rates your burn out of 10.
FIELD MANUAL

How to burn

  • ◈Tap a card, then tap a glowing pyre. If only one pyre fits, it plays instantly. Desktop keys: 1–9 select, Q/W/E choose pyre, S stoke.
  • 🜂Scoring — card played: 10 × flame. Ignition: 60 × flame. Pure Burn: +120 × flame. Ash purged: +40 × flame. Round clear: 120 × round + 8 per card left in deck.
  • ▼The Rite deepens — Round 3+: ASH chokes your hand · Round 4+: VOID joins the elements · Round 6+: pyres demand 6 to ignite (7 at round 9) · Wilds and echoes dwindle to nothing. Watch the banner: it announces every edict.
  • ♻Stoking (drawing) is always allowed while the deck lasts — but it resets your flame and burns your lifeline.
  • ♥Every 3rd pyre cleared rekindles a lost ember. Aim for the Critic's 9.5.
